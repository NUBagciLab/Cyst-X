Overview

A public benchmark for IPMN risk stratification

Intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasms (IPMNs) are pancreatic cancer precursors, and guideline-based risk stratification both over-treats indolent lesions and misses progression. We present Cyst-X, a public benchmark of 1,461 T1-weighted (T1W) and T2-weighted (T2W) MRI volumes from 764 patients at seven centers, with expert pancreas masks, risk labels, splits and reference AI risk-prediction models. PanSegNet segmented the pancreas with Dice of 86.34% (T1W) and 89.26% (T2W) on held-out centers. For the primary task, high-risk versus low-risk IPMN, a 3D DenseNet-121 achieved cross-validated AUCs of 74.59% and 73.45%; complementary broad binary and three-class tasks reached 78.67–81.28% and 73.47–75.76%. Because patient images often cannot be pooled across institutions, we also simulated federated learning across the seven centers. On T2W MRI, federated FedProx was similar to centralized training in center-averaged external AUC (71.00% versus 71.18%) but not in pooled AUC (62.35% versus 77.49%). Radiologist comparisons showed cohort-dependent sensitivity–specificity tradeoffs. Cyst-X supports reproducible method development and identifies within-IPMN risk discrimination and cross-center score comparability as open challenges.

Dataset

Seven centers, two MRI sequences

Scanners span GE, Siemens and Philips at 1.5 T and 3 T. Each volume comes with an expert pancreas mask; risk-labeled cases carry a three-tier label.

Patients per center
High-risk IPMN

High-grade dysplasia or worse, including carcinoma in situ and invasive carcinoma, confirmed by endoscopic-ultrasound-guided biopsy or surgical histopathology.

Low-risk IPMN

Low- or intermediate-grade dysplasia on resection or tissue sampling, or no biopsy and at least three years of stable imaging follow-up (growth below 2.5 mm per year, no worrisome features or high-risk stigmata under the Kyoto criteria).

No-risk pancreas

Normal pancreatic imaging without cystic lesions, or benign cysts not associated with IPMN, as determined on imaging.

Pipeline

From multicenter MRI to risk prediction

Scans are harmonized, the pancreas is segmented with PanSegNet, and the pancreatic region feeds deep-learning and radiomics classifiers trained centrally or across institutional silos with FedAvg and FedProx.

Benchmark 2

IPMN risk classification

The primary clinical task separates high-risk from low-risk IPMN. Broad binary and three-class tasks add the no-risk group. Models are evaluated with patient-level five-fold cross-validation (internal) and leave-one-center-out evaluation (external), with centralized and federated training.

Reader study

Comparison with radiologists

Three blinded abdominal radiologists scored all 629 cases with paired T1W and T2W MRI under imaging-only conditions. Their assessments are compared with radiomics and deep-learning models in three cohorts.

Model values use validation-selected thresholds. Comparisons are exploratory and describe performance at the reported operating points rather than at matched sensitivity or specificity. The radiologist average is the arithmetic mean of the three readers, not a consensus prediction. Model inputs used radiologist-defined pancreas regions of interest.
